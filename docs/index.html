Common questions

Before you start

Is this really free? Who runs it?

Yes, it's completely free. BillBack Baltimore is an independent, open-source project. It isn't run by BGE, the City or the State, and it will never ask you for payment or bank details.

Is it safe to upload my bill?

Your bill is read inside your own browser and never sent to a server. When you close the tab, it's gone. You can also skip the upload and type the numbers in.

Can disputing a bill get my service shut off?

For BGE, filing a billing-dispute complaint with the Maryland PSC pauses shut-offs while it's reviewed. For City water, the law protects continued service while a review is pending. Paying the part you don't dispute is still a good idea.

What if I can't afford the bill even if it's right?

You may qualify for help. EUSP and MEAP help with BGE bills, and Water4All caps water bills by income. Check your eligibility in 30 seconds.

Is this legal advice?

No. It's general information based on published rules, with a link to each source. For legal help, contact Maryland Legal Aid.
