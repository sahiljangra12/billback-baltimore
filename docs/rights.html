How to dispute a bill, step by step

Who to contact, in what order, and the deadlines that matter. Each step links to the official page.

Habits that win disputes

Put it in writing

Calls are fine for a first try, but follow up in writing (email, web form or letter) so there's a dated record.

Log every contact

Date, time, the rep's name and any reference number. You'll need them if you escalate.

Photograph the evidence

Your meter with today's date, the tow lot's sign, your receipt, any damage.

Pay what isn't disputed

Paying the undisputed part of a bill shows good faith and helps protect your service while the dispute runs.
