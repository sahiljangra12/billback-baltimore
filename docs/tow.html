About the City tow

Your rights after a private-lot tow

  1. If you get there before the car leaves the lot, they must release it. The drop fee is at most half a full tow.
  2. The storage lot must take cash or 2+ major cards. A cash-only lot needs a working ATM.
  3. You can pick up your car 6 a.m. to midnight, every day.
  4. Pick up within 48 hours and you owe no notice fee.
  5. The tower needs the lot owner's written authorization and a photo of the violation.

Sources: §21-10A-04, §21-10A-05
