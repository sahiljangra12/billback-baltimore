# Roadmap

## Now (v0.1)
- BGE, water and tow checkers with source-linked findings
- In-browser PDF/photo reading
- Assistance finder, escalation guides, dispute letters

## Next
- **Real-bill test set.** Anonymized BGE and DPW PDFs to harden the parsers.
- **Spanish version.** Large Spanish-speaking communities in Highlandtown and Southeast Baltimore.
- **Parking tickets & speed cameras.** Same model: check the citation, write the contest.
- **Landlord pass-through utilities (RUBS).** Check allocation math in renters' leases.
- **Reminders.** Opt-in deadline reminders (10-day DPW appeal, 90-day WACA window) via calendar file download.

## Later: the business
- **Done-for-you disputes.** Free self-serve stays free. A paid service files the complaint and follows up, with a contingency fee only on money recovered.
- **Partners.** Tenant groups, legal aid, councilmember offices and churches already field these calls. Give them a bulk intake version.
- **Data.** Aggregated, anonymized error patterns (e.g. estimated-read rates by ZIP) as public-interest reporting and advocacy evidence.
