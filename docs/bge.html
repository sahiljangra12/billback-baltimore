Where to find these on a BGE bill

  1. Usage (kWh, therms) and the billing period are in the meter-reading section.
  2. An "E" or the word "Estimated" next to a reading means BGE didn't read your meter.
  3. If the supply line names a company other than BGE, you're with a third-party supplier. Your Price to Compare is printed on the bill.
  4. The usage history chart shows the same month last year.
