Rates we check against

DPW residential rates from July 1, 2026 (FY2027):

  • Water: $4.86 per CCF
  • Sewer: $14.37 per CCF
  • Infrastructure (5/8" meter): $17.52 water + $16.51 sewer / month
  • Account management: $5.79 per bill

Bills for periods before July 2026 are checked at FY2026 rates.

Quick leak test

Turn off every tap, then watch the meter's low-flow indicator for 15 minutes. If it moves, water is escaping somewhere. Toilets are the usual suspect.
